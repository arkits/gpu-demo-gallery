Timbury

Needs the unpacked files of NVIDIA's demo. They stay on this computer.

A dancing fairy, a swarm of bugs, grass and hair: skinned meshes, morph targets, HDR bloom and a clickable in-scene menu.

Drag to orbit, and scroll or pinch to zoom. 1–2 cameras · F free camera · Space pause · M mute · Enter full screen. Click the menu bar in the scene too.

NVIDIA's “Timbury” (BugLooker) demo, 2003.
